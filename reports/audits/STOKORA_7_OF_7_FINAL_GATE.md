## Defect and regression evidence

The live Debezium 3.7.1 first snapshot row was emitted with op=r and source.snapshot=first. On the original candidate 27 of 28 receipts were accepted and one was quarantined as invalid_source. The correction in pipelines/streaming/merge_bronze.py recognizes the initial boundary marker and keeps the operation/metadata compatibility check. The negative test also rejects a numeric marker in place of Boolean true. No format, event-key algorithm, Kafka identity, frozen benchmark or protected checkpoint changed. The qualification reused the genuine Kafka snapshot and rebuilt only in a new isolated path. If an operational environment is ever found to have already quarantined a first record, approve a separately scoped rebuild or reconciliation. Ordinary replay into the same append-only ledger will not silently rewrite a receipt.

New tests are tests/integration/streaming/qualify_initial_snapshot.py and tests/integration/lambda/qualify_snapshot_to_forecast.py. The original 27/28 ledger remains available in the retained isolated volumes. The full raw evidence and fault orchestration live under docs/evidence/final-functional-gate in the engineering workspace. They are outside the publication branch.

Execution details: the corrected isolated Spark runtime ran the existing broker’s real snapshot records through the fixed decoder, with the original rejected records preserved. The first restart attempt used Docker's manual stop semantics and suppressed automatic restart. The retest crashed only the Connect Java worker inside the isolated service and proved the supervisor's restart policy. The Gold harness's new directory initially belonged to the reader group. Its test-only parent was corrected to the jobs group and the partial scratch outputs were retained separately. Neither harness adjustment relaxed the deployed privileges.
